# 8percent (에잇퍼센트)

> 8percent is a Seoul-based fintech company, founded in November 2014, that built Korea's
> first-generation peer-to-peer lending marketplace and in June 2021 became the first company
> registered as an Online Investment-linked Finance Business operator (온라인투자연계금융업,
> registration 2021-2) under the Financial Services Commission. It matches retail and institutional
> investors with mid-credit borrowers across unsecured personal credit loans, real-estate-backed
> lending, stock-purchase loans, gig-worker financing, small-business loans and rental-deposit
> loans, underwriting them with an in-house AI credit-assessment model marketed as "E-index".
> In July 2026 it acquired security-token operator A-Panda Partners to tokenize loan receivables.
